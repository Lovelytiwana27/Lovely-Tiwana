ABOUT ME

I am Lovely Tiwana, the creative mind behind Lovely Tiwana Films. With a carefully crafted portfolio of over 20 completed projects, I specialize in directing, shooting, and editing dynamic visual content.


My approach adapts to the exact needs of the project. Whether I am stepping in exclusively as a precision Video Editor, leading the vision as a Director and Editor, or handling the complete visual journey as a Director, DOP, and Editor all at once, my focus remains on delivering premium cinematic quality. Armed with industry-leading Sony Cinema cameras, I ensure every frame tells a compelling story.
